Business. &#160; Our History &#160; MamaMancini&#8217;s Holdings, Inc. (formerly Mascot Properties, Inc.) (the &#8220;Company&#8221; or &#8220;MamaMancini&#8217;s&#8221;) was incorporated in the State of Nevada on July 22, 2009. Mascot Properties, Inc.&#8217;s (&#8220;Mascot&#8221;) activities since its inception consisted of trying to locate real estate properties to manage, primarily related to student housing, and services which included general property management, maintenance and activities coordination for residents. Mascot did not have any significant development of such business and did not derive any revenue. Due to the lack of results in its attempt to implement its original business plan, management determined it was in the best interests of the shareholders to look for other potential business opportunities. &#160; On February 22, 2010, MamaMancini&#8217;s LLC was formed as a limited liability company under the laws of the state of New Jersey in order to commercialize our initial products. On March 5, 2012, the members of MamaMancini&#8217;s LLC, holders of 4,700 units (the &#8220;Units&#8221;) of MamaMancini&#8217;s LLC, exchanged the Units for 15,000,000 shares of common stock and those certain options to purchase an additional 223,404 shares of MamaMancini&#8217;s Inc. (the &#8220;Exchange&#8221;). Upon consummation of the Exchange, MamaMancini&#8217;s LLC ceased to exist and all further business has been and continues to be conducted by MamaMancini&#8217;s Inc. &#160; On January 24, 2013, Mascot, Mascot Properties Acquisition Corp, a Delaware corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company (&#8220;Merger Sub&#8221;), MamaMancini&#8217;s Inc., a privately-held Delaware Corporation headquartered in New Jersey (&#8220;Mama&#8217;s&#8221;) and David Dreslin, an individual (the &#8220;Majority Shareholder&#8221;), entered into an Acquisition Agreement and Plan of Merger (the &#8220;Agreement&#8221;) pursuant to which the Merger Sub was merged with and into Mama&#8217;s, with Mama&#8217;s surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company (the &#8220;Merger&#8221;). The transaction (the &#8220;Closing&#8221;) took place on January 24, 2013 (the &#8220;Closing Date&#8221;). Mascot acquired, through a reverse triangular merger, all of the outstanding capital stock of Mama&#8217;s in exchange for issuing Mama&#8217;s shareholders (the &#8220;Mama&#8217;s Shareholders&#8221;), pro-rata, a total of 20,054,000 shares of the Company&#8217;s common stock. As a result of the Merger, the Mama&#8217;s Shareholders became the majority shareholders of Mascot. Immediately following the Closing of the Agreement, Mascot changed its business plan to that of Mama&#8217;s. On March 8, 2013, Mascot received notice from the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (&#8220;FINRA&#8221;) that its application to change its name and symbol had been approved and effective Monday, March 11, 2013, Mascot began trading under its new name, &#8220;MamaMancini&#8217;s Holdings, Inc.&#8221; and under its new symbol, &#8220;MMMB&#8221;. &#160; On November 1, 2017, MamaMancini&#8217;s, Joseph Epstein Food Enterprises, Inc., a New Jersey corporation (&#8220;JEFE&#8221;), and MMMB Acquisition, Inc., a Nevada corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of MamaMancini&#8217;s (&#8220;Merger Sub&#8221;), completed a merger transaction whereby JEFE merged with and into Merger Sub, with Merger Sub continuing as the surviving entity and a wholly owned subsidiary of MamaMancini&#8217;s. Under the terms of the Merger Agreement and in connection with the merger, the Company acquired all assets of JEFE. The consideration for the transaction was (a) the extinguishment of the Inter-Company Loan between the parties, (b) the assumption by the Company of all JEFE accounts payable and accrued expenses (c) assumption by the Company of certain third-party loans to JEFE totaling approximately $782,000 and (d) indemnification of Carl Wolf with respect to his collateralization of a bank loan to JEFE in the amount of approximately $250,000. As a result of the transaction, (i) the Company became the sole shareholder of JEFE, which became a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company.
