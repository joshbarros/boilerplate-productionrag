# Evaluation of vancomycin utilization in medical and pediatric wards: a retrospective cross-sectional study at Bule Hora University Teaching Hospital, Ethiopia.

**Authors:** Gerina, Elema, Fikadu, Amanual
**Journal:** BMC infectious diseases (2025)
**PMID:** 41174547
**Source:** PubMed Central (open access)

## Abstract

Vancomycin is a critical antibiotic used for serious Gram‑positive infections, particularly methicillin‑resistant Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA). However, inappropriate use contributes to antimicrobial resistance (AMR). Limited evidence exists on the extent and appropriateness of vancomycin utilization in Ethiopian hospitals.
