# All in on ARBs: Is it time to fold the ACEIs?

**Authors:** Phan, Pham, Yang, Lee, Dugré, +1 more
**Journal:** Canadian family physician Medecin de famille canadien (2026)
**PMID:** 41844292
**Source:** PubMed Central (open access)

## Abstract

To summarize the efficacy and safety of angiotensin II receptor blockers (ARBs) compared to angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors (ACEIs) in primary hypertension and other compelling indications, focusing on cardiovascular and renal outcomes.
