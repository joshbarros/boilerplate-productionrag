# Corticosteroids as adjunctive therapy in acute meningitis: a narrative review.

**Authors:** Ullah, Khan, Haseeb, Ahmad, Abbas, +5 more
**Journal:** Annals of medicine and surgery (2012) (2026)
**PMID:** 41675892
**Source:** PubMed Central (open access)

## Abstract

Acute meningitis is a life-threatening neurological emergency with substantial global morbidity and mortality. Adjunctive corticosteroid therapy aims to mitigate the inflammatory response and improve clinical outcomes.
