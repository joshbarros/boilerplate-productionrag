# Metformin Sensitizes HR+/HER2- Breast Cancer Cells to CDK4/6 Inhibitor via Suppressing of PI3K/AKT/mTOR Signaling Pathway.

**Authors:** Zhu, Yang, Li, Wang, Zhang, +4 more
**Journal:** Drug design, development and therapy (2026)
**PMID:** 42415939
**Source:** PubMed Central (open access)

## Abstract

CDK4/6 (Cyclin-dependent kinase 4/6) inhibitors are recommended as the first-line treatment for Hormone receptor-positive/human epidermal growth receptor 2-negative (HR+/HER2-) breast cancer, but their efficacy is limited. Metformin, a widely used antidiabetic drug, has demonstrated antitumor potential and can enhance the efficacy of various cancer therapies. Nevertheless, its specific role in sensitizing HR+/HER2- breast cancer to CDK4/6 inhibition and the underlying mechanisms remain poorly defined.
